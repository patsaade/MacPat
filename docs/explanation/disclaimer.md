# Disclaimer

MacPat deletes files and turns off things that start on their own. Treat it that way.

- **Back up first.** Time Machine or anything else. I mean it.
- **No warranty.** The [MIT license](../../LICENSE) says it is provided "as is". I can't promise it won't remove something you wanted.
- **You decide.** MacPat shows you what it found and why. You choose what runs. What you confirm is on you.
- **Persistence items can matter.** Disabling or removing one can stop an app, a VPN, a backup, or a sync tool from working. Disable first, and remove later.
- **Caches can bite.** Apps rebuild caches, but a running app may misbehave if its cache disappears. Quit apps first.
- **Space isn't freed yet.** Files go to the Trash. Empty it to get the space back. Local snapshots may hold it a while longer.
- **Not a security product.** Unusual doesn't mean malicious, and normal doesn't mean safe. See [not a malware scan](not-a-malware-scan.md).
- **Not Apple's.** MacPat is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple Inc. Mac and macOS are Apple trademarks. Apple's trademark guidance discourages "Mac" in product names. The name may have to change before a public release.
- **Not advice.** Nothing here promises a faster Mac.
