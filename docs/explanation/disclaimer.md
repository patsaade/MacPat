# Disclaimer

MacPat deletes files. Treat it that way.

- **Back up first.** Time Machine or anything else. I mean it.
- **No warranty.** The [MIT license](../../LICENSE) says it is provided "as is". I cannot promise it will not remove something you wanted.
- **You decide.** MacPat shows you what it found. You choose what moves. What you confirm is on you.
- **Caches can bite.** Apps rebuild caches, but a running app may misbehave if you remove its cache. Quit apps first.
- **Space is not freed yet.** Files go to the Trash. Empty it to get the space.
- **Not Apple's.** MacPat is not affiliated with or endorsed by Apple Inc. Mac and macOS are Apple trademarks.
- **Not advice.** Nothing here promises a faster Mac. Cleaning caches often just makes apps rebuild them.
