# Verdicts

Every step carries one. It is shown as a word and a glyph, never as color alone.

| Verdict | Means | Ticked after a scan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ✓ SAFE | Comes back on its own, or does nothing now. Examples are caches, logs, and orphaned jobs. | Usually |
| ! REVIEW | Probably fine, but it costs something or the match is a guess. Read the reason. | No, with a few marked exceptions |
| 🔒 LOCKED | Shown only. MacPat won't run it. Examples are Apple items, running apps, and places too risky to touch. | Never |

A verdict is MacPat's best judgment from what it can see. It is not a promise.
