# Your first clean

You will scan, read a preview, and move your first files to the Trash. Five minutes.

1. Open MacPat. If a banner asks for Full Disk Access, click **Open Settings**, switch MacPat on, and come back.
2. Click **Clean**. It scans on its own. Safe groups start ticked. Review groups never do.
3. Click the arrow on **Caches and logs: Logs**. Each file shows its path, why it's there, and what happens if it goes.
4. Untick everything except **Logs**.
5. Click **Preview**. A hatched **Dry run** banner says what would happen. Nothing has moved.
6. Tick **Show commands**. Each step shows the shell line it stands for.
7. Click **Move to Trash…** and confirm.
8. Open the Trash. Your files are there. Put them back if you want.

The space comes back when you empty the Trash. MacPat will not do that for you.
