# Your first clean

You will scan, preview, and move your first files to the Trash. Five minutes.

1. Open MacPat. If a banner asks for Full Disk Access, click **Open Settings**, switch MacPat on, and come back.
2. Click **Clean** in the sidebar. MacPat scans on its own. Click **Scan** to run it again.
3. Each row is a group of files with its size. Tick **Logs**.
4. Click **Preview**. The footer says what would move. Nothing has moved yet.
5. Click **Move to Trash…** and confirm.
6. Open the Trash and look. Your files are there. Put them back if you want.

The space comes back when you empty the Trash. MacPat will not do that for you.
