# Find what filled the disk

1. Open **Analyze**. The top box shows used, free, and what macOS can purge on its own.
2. In **Space**, click a folder to go in. **Up** goes back. Sizes are allocated sizes.
3. Right-click anything for **Reveal in Finder** or **Add to Trash Plan**. Only items inside Desktop, Documents, Downloads, Movies, Music, Pictures, Developer, or Public can be added.
4. **Large and old** lists files over 500 MB that nobody opened in 180 days, plus anything you added. Preview and move as usual.

If free space doesn't grow after you empty the Trash, local Time Machine snapshots may still hold the files. They expire on their own. **Optimize** can delete them now.
