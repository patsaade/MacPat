# Privacy

MacPat collects nothing and sends nothing. It has no network code, no analytics, no crash reporter, and no account.

It reads file names and sizes on your Mac to build a list. That list stays in memory and is gone when you quit.

It stores no settings yet. When it does, they will live on your Mac only.

Updates come from Homebrew, not from the app.

Full Disk Access is needed to see every cache folder. macOS asks you for it. You can take it back at any time in System Settings.
